**An End-to-End Data Analytics Project Using SQL, Python, and Power BI**

## Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world analytics engagement for a fictional UK-based gifting and home décor e-commerce business, **IKEA Gifting**.
The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights by identifying high-value customers, understanding purchasing behavior, and detecting customers at risk of churn.
The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data extraction and cleaning to customer segmentation and interactive dashboard development.
It demonstrates how **SQL, Python, and Power BI** can be integrated into a single analytics workflow to solve business problems and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

The business lacks a structured understanding of customer value and purchasing behavior. Without customer segmentation, retention strategies are largely reactive rather than targeted.
The key challenge is to:

- Identify the most valuable customers.
- Understand how much revenue each customer segment contributes.
- Identify inactive customers who may be at risk of churning.
- Understand purchasing trends across different time periods and geographic markets.
- Develop targeted retention strategies to improve customer engagement and revenue.

## Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer the following questions:

1. Who are the most valuable customers, and how much revenue do they contribute?
2. Which customers have become inactive and are at risk of churning?
3. How does customer value and retention change across months, quarters, and years?
4. How do customer value and churn patterns differ across geographic markets?
5. What actionable strategies can the business implement for each customer segment?

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation – SQL

- Explored the raw transactional dataset and examined its structure.
- Identified and handled missing and invalid values.
- Removed duplicate records and irrelevant data.
- Filtered transactions to ensure meaningful customer purchase analysis.
- Prepared a clean dataset for further analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis & RFM Segmentation – Python

Performed exploratory data analysis using Pandas and NumPy to understand customer purchasing patterns.

Implemented **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis** to evaluate customer value:

- **Recency:** Number of days since the customer's last purchase.
- **Frequency:** Total number of purchases made by the customer.
- **Monetary:** Total revenue generated by the customer.

Customers were assigned RFM scores and grouped into meaningful customer segments based on their purchasing behavior.

Key segments include:
- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- New Customers
- At Risk
- Cannot Lose Them
- Hibernating
- Lost

### 3. Dashboard Development – Power BI

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key business metrics and customer insights.

The dashboard focuses on:

- Customer distribution across RFM segments.
- Revenue contribution by customer segment.
- Customer churn and retention analysis.
- Customer value across geographic markets.
- Monthly, quarterly, and yearly revenue trends.
- Identification of high-value and at-risk customers.

## Key Findings & Business Insights

- **Revenue Concentration:** Champions account for approximately 22% of the customer base but contribute close to **68% of total revenue**, highlighting a strong concentration of revenue among high-value customers.
- **Customer Churn:** Overall churn stands at **50.86%**, based on a 90-day inactivity threshold, indicating a significant customer retention challenge.
- **Customer Value:** Average customer value varies substantially across segments, ranging from a few hundred among Lost customers to several thousand among Champions.
- **Seasonality:** Revenue demonstrates noticeable seasonal spikes during Q4, which is consistent with purchasing patterns commonly associated with gifting and home décor products.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can help improve customer retention and revenue:

| Customer Segment | Recommended Strategy |
|---|---|
| Champions | Reward with exclusive offers, loyalty benefits, and early access to new products. |
| Loyal Customers | Encourage repeat purchases through personalized recommendations and loyalty programs. |
| Potential Loyalists | Use targeted promotions to convert them into loyal customers. |
| New Customers | Introduce welcome offers and personalized product recommendations. |
| At Risk | Launch re-engagement campaigns and limited-time discounts. |
| Cannot Lose Them | Prioritize personalized outreach and high-value retention offers. |
| Hibernating | Use targeted win-back campaigns to encourage customers to return. |
| Lost | Test low-cost reactivation campaigns rather than investing heavily in retention. |
